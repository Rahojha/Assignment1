<h1>My Recipe Book</h1>

<h2>Vegetable Sandwich</h2>

<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1528735602780-2552fd46c7af"
     alt="Vegetable sandwich" width="350">

<p><b>Preparation Time:</b> 10 minutes</p>
<p><b>Cooking Time:</b> 5 minutes</p>
<p><b>Servings:</b> 2 people</p>

<h3>Ingredients</h3>
<ul>
    <li>4 slices of bread</li>
    <li>1 small tomato</li>
    <li>1 small cucumber</li>
    <li>1 small onion</li>
    <li>2 tablespoons butter</li>
    <li>Salt and pepper to taste</li>
    <li>Green chutney (optional)</li>
</ul>

<h3>Cooking Steps</h3>
<ol>
    <li>Wash and slice the vegetables.</li>
    <li>Spread butter or green chutney on the bread.</li>
    <li>Add tomato, cucumber, and onion slices.</li>
    <li>Sprinkle salt and pepper.</li>
    <li>Cover with another slice of bread.</li>
    <li>Toast in a pan until golden, if desired.</li>
    <li>Cut and serve fresh.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Serving Suggestion</h3>
<p>Serve with tomato ketchup or green chutney.</p>
